Thanks for your interest in contributing to this project!

If you're interested in contributing a non-trivial feature or bug-fix, please open an issue first. If you have questions, feel free to open a discussion thread.

Please make sure to follow the [Code of Conduct](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

### AI Policy

Commits must not be authored or co-authored by AI. Prose and communication should not be AI-generated. AI can be used for translation and accessibility purposes, but please include the [original untranslated text](https://docs.github.com/en/get-started/writing-on-github/working-with-advanced-formatting/organizing-information-with-collapsed-sections) as well.

### Ownership

*Code copied from `rust-lang/rust` is exempt from the following.*

As a contributor, you are responsible for everything you submit. You must personally understand every line of code or documentation that you put forward for review, and be able to articulate the design rationale and implementation tradeoffs when asked. Work within your skill level and knowledge of the project.
